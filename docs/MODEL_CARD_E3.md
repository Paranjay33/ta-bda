## Intended use

Research and teaching demonstration of sentence-level 3-class sentiment (negative / neutral / positive) for Romanized Hindi-English code-mixed social-media text, as part of the HinglishSense E1–E4 comparison.

## Out of scope

Decisions about individuals (moderation, hiring, credit, health, legal); measuring anyone's real feelings; Devanagari or non-Hinglish text; long documents; any use that treats the score as ground truth.

## Limitations and bias

- Pretrained on generic multilingual CommonCrawl, not on Romanized Hindi-English code-mixing.
- Fine-tuned on stemmed, stop-word-filtered text; raw input is mapped into that space at serving time.
- English negators and contrast words (`not`, `no`, `but`) were removed from the data upstream, so no model can use them; Hindi ones (`nahi`, `mat`, `lekin`) remain.
- Tweets from one collection period; political and religious topics are heavily represented, and the neutral class is noisy. Errors are not uniform across topics.
- One split and one seed; no estimate of seed variance.
